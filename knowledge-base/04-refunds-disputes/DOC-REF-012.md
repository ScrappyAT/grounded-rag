# Domestic and International Refund Policy v2.0
> **Document ID:** `DOC-REF-012` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-10 | **Status:** ACTIVE
> **Supersedes:** `DOC-REF-002`

## Section 1: Policy Background and Revision History

The 2024 Policy (v1.0) was replaced because intermediary correspondent banking delays caused customer disputes. Under the 2026 Policy (v2.0), international wire refunds were realistically extended from 3-5 days to 14 business days.

## Section 3: Standard Refund Windows and Settlement Periods (v2.0)

Under the active 2026 Refund Policy (v2.0, DOC-REF-012), the standard refund period and processing execution window mandates that domestic card refunds are processed within 5 business days, whereas international transfers require up to 14 business days due to intermediary correspondent banking clearing schedules. This defines the authoritative ApexPay refund period and timeline across all corporate systems, strictly superseding all prior timelines or unauthorized override bulletins. Merchants must ensure adequate settlement balances before refund issuance.

## Section 5: Express Refund Program

The Express Merchant Refund program allows eligible enterprise merchants with pre-funded reserve balances to credit customer accounts within 24 hours, subject to a 0.5% express fee.
