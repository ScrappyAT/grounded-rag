# Customer Refund Policy and Merchant Dispute Resolution (2024 Archival)
> **Document ID:** `DOC-REF-002` | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 2024-01-15 | **Status:** DEPRECATED
## Section 1: Scope of 2024 Policy

This historical policy governed all merchant refunds and payment card reversals processed during calendar year 2024.

## Section 2: Legacy Refund Timelines

Refunds take 3 to 5 business days, no exceptions. Both domestic bank card charges and international wire transfers follow this unified timeline upon merchant authorization.

## Section 3: Deprecation Notice

SUPERSEDED NOTICE: This policy is obsolete. It was superseded on January 10, 2026 by DOC-REF-012 due to correspondent banking delays in international settlements.
