# Card Scheme Chargeback Rebuttal Procedures & Evidence Windows
> **Document ID:** `DOC-REF-014` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-12 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Dispute Initiation

Customers may raise payment disputes through their issuing bank under Visa, Mastercard, or local card network rules.

## Section 2: Chargeback Evidence Windows

Merchants must submit documentary evidence (such as proof of delivery, service logs, or signed invoices) within 7 calendar days of the dispute notification. Failure to upload evidence within this 7-day window results in automated default forfeiture of the disputed funds.

## Section 3: Acceptable Documentary Proof

Proof of fulfillment must clearly identify customer name, delivery address, carrier tracking timestamps, and IP addresses recorded at checkout.
