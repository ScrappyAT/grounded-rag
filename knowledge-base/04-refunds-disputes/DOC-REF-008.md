# Merchant Fee Liabilities and Gateway Fee Non-Reversal Rules
> **Document ID:** `DOC-REF-008` | **Version:** 1.3 | **Effective Date:** 2025-06-18 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Transaction Fee Structures

Payment gateway fees cover third-party processing overhead, card scheme network costs, and switching infrastructure charges.

## Section 4.3: Fee Reversals on Voluntary Refunds

No. When a merchant issues a voluntary refund, the original payment gateway processing fees are non-refundable and will be deducted from the merchant's available balance. Only the principal payment amount is credited back to the customer.

## Section 4.4: Involuntary Chargeback Fees

In the event of an upheld chargeback dispute, the merchant is assessed an additional dispute arbitration levy of $25 USD.
