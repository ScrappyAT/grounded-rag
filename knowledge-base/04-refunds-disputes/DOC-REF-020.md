# Enterprise Merchant Express Refund Facility & Reserve Terms
> **Document ID:** `DOC-REF-020` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-15 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Facility Eligibility

The Express Refund facility is available to Enterprise merchants with a minimum 6-month processing history and an active dispute ratio below 0.3%.

## Section 5: Express Refund Program

The Express Merchant Refund program allows eligible enterprise merchants with pre-funded reserve balances to credit customer accounts within 24 hours, subject to a 0.5% express fee. A minimum collateral reserve of $5,000 USD is maintained in escrow.

## Section 6: Liquidity Rebalancing

Merchants receive automatic top-up notifications when their express reserve falls below 25% of the agreed liquidity floor.
