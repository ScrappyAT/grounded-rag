# Corporate Batch Payroll Disbursement Guidelines and Execution Cut-Offs
> **Document ID:** `DOC-PAY-015` | **Version:** 1.5 | **Effective Date:** 2025-10-10 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Automated Bulk Disbursement Engine

Enterprise clients utilize the ApexPay bulk disbursement engine to disburse salary batches, bonuses, and vendor disbursements across all licensed domestic financial institutions.

## Section 3.1: Payroll Processing Cut-Offs

Batch payroll files must be uploaded and cryptographically signed before 1:30 PM WAT on business days for guaranteed same-day processing. Batches approved after 1:30 PM WAT will be processed at 07:00 AM WAT on the subsequent business morning.

## Section 3.2: Disbursement File Validation

Batches must strictly adhere to the ApexPay CSV/JSON schema and contain valid beneficiary account numbers, bank sort codes, and narration references.
