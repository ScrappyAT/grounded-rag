# Cross-Border Inbound Wire Processing and SWIFT Fee Schedules
> **Document ID:** `DOC-PAY-008` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-05 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: International Transfer Rails

Cross-border incoming transactions are accepted via SWIFT network routing through ApexPay correspondent partner institutions in New York and London.

## Section 4: Cross-Border Wire Fees

Inbound SWIFT wire transfers incur a flat fee of $15 USD plus a 1.2% foreign exchange conversion spread over the interbank benchmark rate. Correspondent banking intermediary deductions may apply depending on the sending institution.

## Section 5: Currency Conversion Cut-Offs

Foreign currency exchanges are executed at the prevailing mid-market fixing rate updated hourly between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM WAT.
