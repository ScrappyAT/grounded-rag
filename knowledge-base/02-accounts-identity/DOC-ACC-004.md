# Tier 2 Individual Account Verification Limits and Transaction Thresholds
> **Document ID:** `DOC-ACC-004` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Tier 2 KYC Requirements

Tier 2 individual verification requires validation of Bank Verification Number (BVN) or National Identity Number (NIN), a confirmed mobile phone number, and a verified residential address.

## Section 3: Tier 2 Account Limits

The single-transaction debit limit for a Tier 2 individual account is 200,000 NGN (or 250 USD equivalent), with a maximum daily cumulative limit of 1,000,000 NGN. Inward credit transactions are capped at 2,000,000 NGN per calendar day.

## Section 4: Upgrade Pathway to Tier 3

To exceed Tier 2 thresholds, account holders must upload a valid government-issued photographic ID and a utility bill dated within the last 90 days.
