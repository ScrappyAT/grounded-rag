# Tier 3 Corporate Merchant Identity & Beneficial Ownership Verification
> **Document ID:** `DOC-ACC-011` | **Version:** 3.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-02-01 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Corporate KYC Framework

ApexPay complies with international anti-money laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorism Financing (CFT) guidelines governing corporate merchant accounts.

## Section 2.1: Corporate Documentation Requirements

Acceptable documents include a valid International Passport, National Identity Card (NIN slip with verifiable QR), or Driver's License for all directors holding over 25% equity, alongside the CAC Certificate of Incorporation. In addition, certified Memorandum and Articles of Association (MEMART) and a corporate tax identification number (TIN) must be submitted.

## Section 2.2: Sanctions and PEP Screening

All beneficial owners undergo automated checks against OFAC, PEP, and international sanctions registries before account clearance.
