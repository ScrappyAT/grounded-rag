# Production Infrastructure Maintenance Schedules & Client Notice SLA
> **Document ID:** `DOC-OPS-012` | **Version:** 1.5 | **Effective Date:** 2025-11-05 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Maintenance Classifications

Maintenance is categorized into routine scheduled maintenance and critical emergency security patches.

## Section 2: Scheduled Maintenance Windows

Routine maintenance windows occur on Sundays between 02:00 AM and 04:00 AM WAT. Merchants must receive at least 72 hours advance notification via email and the status page. Gateway routing is automatically switched to secondary availability zones.

## Section 3: Emergency Patches

Critical zero-day security vulnerabilities may be patched without advance notice, with post-maintenance debriefs published within 4 hours.
