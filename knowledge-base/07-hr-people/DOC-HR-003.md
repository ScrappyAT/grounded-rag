# Remote Work Infrastructure, Hardware Stipends and Office Equipment
> **Document ID:** `DOC-HR-003` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Remote First Principles

ApexPay operates a distributed-first engineering and product organization across African and international hubs.

## Section 2: Home Office Setup Allowance

Full-time engineering and product employees receive a one-time onboarding setup stipend of $1,000 USD (or local currency equivalent), plus an ongoing annual equipment refresh budget of $500 USD. Receipts must be uploaded to the HR portal within 30 days of purchase.

## Section 3: Company Hardware Provisioning

All engineering personnel are issued a standardized corporate MacBook Pro or ThinkPad workstation with encrypted disk storage.
