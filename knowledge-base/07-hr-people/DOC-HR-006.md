# Employee Paid Time Off, Annual Leave Accrual and Holiday Policies
> **Document ID:** `DOC-HR-006` | **Version:** 1.8 | **Effective Date:** 2025-10-01 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 paid working days of annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 2.08 days per completed month of service. A maximum of 5 unused leave days may be carried over into the first quarter of the following year.

## Section 2: Public Holidays

Employees observe all statutory national holidays recognized in their country of legal residence or employment contract.

## Section 3: Compassionate and Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, while secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid leave.
