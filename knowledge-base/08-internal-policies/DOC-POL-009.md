# Anti-Bribery, Business Hospitality and Corporate Gift Thresholds
> **Document ID:** `DOC-POL-009` | **Version:** 2.1 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-25 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Compliance with Anti-Corruption Laws

ApexPay strictly complies with the UK Bribery Act, US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA), and relevant national anti-corruption regulations.

## Section 2: Gift and Hospitality Thresholds

Employees may accept modest promotional gifts valued at up to $50 USD (or equivalent). Any gift exceeding $50 USD must be logged in the corporate Gift Register and approved by Compliance. Under no circumstances may cash, gift cards, or negotiable instruments be accepted.

## Section 3: Entertainment and Meals

Occasional business meals within standard commercial moderation are permissible without formal registration if valued under $100 USD per person.
