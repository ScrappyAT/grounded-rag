# Corporate Code of Conduct: Secondary Employment & Moonlighting Policy
> **Document ID:** `DOC-POL-005` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-10 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Duty of Loyalty

Employees owe a fiduciary duty of loyalty and confidentiality to ApexPay Technologies throughout the duration of their employment.

## Section 3: Conflict of Interest and Moonlighting

Employees may engage in non-competing external work only if pre-approved in writing by the Head of People and their manager, provided it is conducted outside working hours and does not utilize company hardware or IP. Consulting for direct fintech competitors or payment processors is strictly prohibited.

## Section 4: Open Source Contributions

Contributing to open source projects that do not disclose proprietary algorithms is encouraged with standard engineering manager sign-off.
