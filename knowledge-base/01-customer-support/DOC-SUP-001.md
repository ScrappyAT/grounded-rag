# Retail Customer Support Operating Hours & Multi-Channel Access
> **Document ID:** `DOC-SUP-001` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-05 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1.1: Channel Overview

ApexPay offers multi-channel support services tailored to user account tiers, including self-service portals, live in-app messaging, email ticketing, and dedicated account manager channels.

## Section 1.2: Support Hours and Availability

Standard retail users have access to email and in-app chat support from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM WAT, Monday through Saturday. Phone support is reserved exclusively for Priority and Enterprise accounts. Urgent tickets logged outside operational hours are queued for first triage at 8:00 AM WAT the following morning.

## Section 1.3: Priority Escalations

Priority retail accounts gain extended chat access until 11:00 PM WAT daily. Dedicated phone support lines are unlisted and provisioned directly within the Enterprise client dashboard.
