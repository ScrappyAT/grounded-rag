# Enterprise Customer Support Service Level Agreement (SLA)
> **Document ID:** `DOC-SUP-003` | **Version:** 2.1 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-15 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Tier Classifications

Support inquiries are categorized into Tier 1 (critical payment disruption or service outage), Tier 2 (billing disputes and settlement delays), and Tier 3 (general account inquiries and API integration assistance).

## Section 2: Enterprise Response SLAs

Tier 1 tickets for Enterprise merchants have a guaranteed first-response SLA of under 15 minutes, available 24/7. Tier 2 tickets require a first response within 2 hours during business hours, while Tier 3 inquiries must be addressed within 12 hours.

## Section 3: SLA Penalty & Credits

If ApexPay fails to meet the 15-minute response SLA for Enterprise Tier 1 tickets for more than three occurrences within a calendar month, the merchant receives a 10% credit against monthly gateway processing fees.
