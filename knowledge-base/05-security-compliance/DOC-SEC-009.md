# Data Security Incident Classification, Escalation and DPO Reporting
> **Document ID:** `DOC-SEC-009` | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2025-12-01 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Incident Severity Matrix

Incidents are graded from P1 (critical data breach or compromise of financial records) to P4 (minor internal policy deviation).

## Section 2.2: Incident Escalation Timelines

Any suspected high-severity data incident must be reported to the DPO and Security Operations team within 2 hours of initial triage, and regulatory notification must occur within 72 hours under NDPR/GDPR rules. The Security Operations Center operates 24/7 incident response channels.

## Section 3: Regulatory Filings

Formal incident reports to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) must include the scope of data affected, containment measures, and remediation plans.
