# Customer Record Retention Schedules & Anti-Money Laundering Compliance
> **Document ID:** `DOC-SEC-015` | **Version:** 1.8 | **Effective Date:** 2025-09-30 | **Status:** ACTIVE
## Section 1: Regulatory Foundation

ApexPay adheres to Central Bank statutory directives and Financial Action Task Force (FATF) record-keeping guidelines.

## Section 4: Data Retention Schedules

Customer transaction records and KYC verification documentation must be securely retained for 7 years following account closure in compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) statutory requirements. Following the 7-year statutory period, data is scheduled for cryptographically verified permanent erasure.

## Section 5: Cold Storage Archiving

Inactive customer records older than 18 months are archived into encrypted cold storage vaults with multi-party authorization keys.
